Privacy Policy for Evolife

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Evolife ("the app") and its maker Lootvoet BV ("we"), enterprise number 0800.934.047 in Belgium, handle information when you play. Evolife is a single-player idle game. We do not run any server, and the app itself does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

Information processed by third parties

Evolife uses Google services to show optional rewarded ads and to sell optional in-app purchases. These services may process limited data:

  • Google AdMob (ads). When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier and basic device/network information to serve and measure ads. See Google's policy and how Google uses data.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases). Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Google Play In-App Review (ratings). Evolife may show Google Play's rating card. Your rating and review go to Google Play under your Google account; we only see them the way any public Play review is visible.
  • Google Play services may collect diagnostics/crash information as part of the platform.

We do not receive any personally identifiable information from these services.

Data stored on your device only

  • Your game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.
  • Local notifications (optional reminders) are scheduled on your device; no data leaves the device.

Feedback by email

The "Send feedback" button in Settings opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us; it pre-fills the app version, your current species and your device platform, which you can edit or remove. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use your email address and message only to reply and to improve the game, and we do not share them.

Children

Evolife is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

  • You can reset your local advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • You can disable notifications in the app's Settings or your device settings. Evolife asks for the notification permission when you send your first expedition.
  • You can clear all local data via the app's "Reset all progress" setting or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
